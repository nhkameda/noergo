# noergo name and logo policy

This is a policy, not legal advice. It explains how you may use the noergo name and
logo. If something is not covered, ask in an issue before you rely on it.

## Code and brand are licensed differently

- The **code** is licensed under the [GNU AGPL v3.0 only](../LICENSE). You may use,
  study, change and share it under that license.
- The **name "noergo"**, the **logo and symbol** (the files in this `brand/` folder)
  and the visual identity are **not** licensed by the AGPL. The AGPL gives you rights
  to the code, not to the brand.

## What you may do

- **Self-host for yourself or your organization.** An unmodified deployment of the
  official image or of this source may keep the noergo name and logo, as long as it
  says it runs noergo and links to <https://github.com/nhkameda/noergo> (for example
  in the footer or the about page).
- **Refer to the project** truthfully by name: "built on noergo", "a fork of
  noergo", "compatible with noergo", in articles, talks, tutorials and comparisons.
- **Use the logo to link to the project**, unaltered, in docs, blog posts or a list
  of tools.

## What needs a different name

- **Forks offered as a public service** (hosted for people outside your own
  organization, free or paid) must use a different name and a different logo, and
  must not suggest they are noergo or endorsed by it. Keep the AGPL notices and say
  the fork is based on noergo, with a link back.
- **Modified versions you distribute** (images, packages, app-store builds) follow
  the same rule: rename and replace the logo.
- Do not use "noergo", or a confusingly similar name, logo or domain, for another
  product, a company name, or a domain name.

## Questions

Open an issue on <https://github.com/nhkameda/noergo>. When in doubt, rename: it is
the quickest way to stay clear of the brand.
